Dream it. Think it through. Do it. Win.

Every idea follows the same four stages, and you are notified each time it moves.

Dream

Write up your idea in a simple form. Save a draft and attach supporting files when you are ready.

Think

Evaluation teams score your idea against clear criteria, level by level, and an adjudicator makes the overall call.

Do

If evaluators ask for changes, you reply or resubmit. Each version is kept so nothing is lost.

Win

Approved ideas move to recognition and award, and the outcome is recorded for everyone to see.

Everything an idea needs, in one place

Built around what CEDA asked for, so each part of the process is visible and accountable.

Simple idea submission

Required fields guide you so nothing is missed. Save drafts, upload files, and see all your submissions in one list.

Multi-level evaluation

Teams review together, score against criteria that CEDA can customise, and pass ideas up to adjudication.

Live status tracking

Dashboards show every idea through submission, review, feedback, approval and award, for staff and committee members.

Feedback and resubmission

Reports for decisions

Participation, outcomes and timelines in dashboards that can be exported to CSV.

Built to grow

New criteria, roles, analytics and links to other tools can be added without rebuilding the portal.

The right access for each person

Permissions are enforced on every request, so people see only what their role allows.

Submitter

Submits ideas, responds to feedback and follows progress.

Evaluator

Reviews assigned ideas, scores them and gives feedback.

Adjudicator

Reviews the scores and records the final decision.

Administrator

Manages content, forms, criteria, users and stages.

Committee observer

Follows live dashboards with read-only access.

Your ideas and your data stay protected

The portal is designed to follow Botswana’s Data Protection Act, 2024.

  • Data held in Botswana
  • Access limited by role, with every action recorded in an audit trail
  • Data used only for the stated purpose and kept only as long as needed
  • Passwords you can change and recover, with one-time codes by email or SMS
  • Updates by email, SMS or in the portal, free of charge
  • Works on Edge, Chrome, Firefox and Safari, on phone or computer

Questions people ask

Short answers to the most common questions.

Who can submit an idea?

Anyone can create an account with an email address or phone number and submit an idea.

How will I know what is happening to my idea?

Your submissions list shows the current stage. You can also subscribe, free of charge, to alerts whenever there is an update.

What if evaluators ask for changes?

You will get a notification with their feedback. Reply in the portal or edit and resubmit. Earlier versions are kept.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Choose “Forgot password” on the sign-in page and follow the steps sent to your email or phone.

Can I get help while submitting?

Yes. Use live chat between 09:00 and 16:00 on working days.
